Asterfall 0.3 — Lantern Garden presentation study

**Extract Asterfall-Windows.zip into a NEW folder and launch Asterfall.exe. Java is included; existing saves remain outside the game folder.**

This release replaces the camera, movement/attack control, render path and main HUD before adding content. It is intentionally one compact test garden: one hero, one enemy type, one destructible crate, one chest and one building, with raised grassy ground, cliffs, a walkable ramp, trees, bushes, fences, flowers and ruins.

- Lower, tighter perspective camera with a much larger player, damped follow and look-ahead.
- Acceleration/braking, interpolated facing, blended stride/idle poses, eased dodge, attack anticipation and lunge, timed contact, hit-stop, recoil and enemy knockback.
- Toon shading, rim light, softened directional shadows, depth-based ambient occlusion/contact darkening, fog, grading and subtle bloom. Cool night lighting contrasts with warm local lanterns.
- Continuous sword trail, flashes, sparks, camera impulses and sound hooks.
- Compact bottom HUD, collapsible garden notes, and nearby rarity-colored loot names that avoid overlap and the hero.

WASD moves directly. Left click approaches/attacks; Shift + left click stands and slashes. Right mouse walks. Space dodges. F interacts/collects/rests. Q/E retain the existing sword skills. I opens equipment, Esc pauses. **N resets the practice garden.**

The attached movement GIF and day/night PNGs are real game captures. 186 controller/layout checks, 28 existing checks and graphics-context combat/render regressions pass. This is an original stylized prototype, not a claim of Dragon Quest Builders production asset quality. Windows/GPU playtesting remains necessary.
