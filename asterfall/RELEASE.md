Asterfall 0.2 — Combat fix and 3D rebuild

**Download Asterfall-Windows.zip, extract the entire ZIP into a new folder, and launch Asterfall.exe. Java is included. Your existing save is kept outside the game folder.**

- Fixes ConcurrentModificationException when a projectile kill triggers a level-up. The exact failure is reproduced in a graphics-context regression, including 60 repeated level-ups.
- Visible full-length sword, shield, armor, sculpted anime face, and articulated swing. Melee damage lands during the animation. Q is Sword Wave; E is Whirlwind.
- Diagonal orthographic overhead camera with camera-relative movement.
- Original textured green grass, masonry courtyards, cottages, ruins, foliage, and directional shadows. Inspired by the requested chunky anime modeling direction; no Dragon Quest artwork or models used.
- Local time, weather for 11355, loot, equipment, talents, and saves retained.

Windows launcher is cross-built; please share launch-log.txt if your Windows playtest finds a problem.
