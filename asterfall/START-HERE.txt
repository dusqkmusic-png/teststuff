ASTERFALL 0.3 — LANTERN GARDEN

WINDOWS 10/11, 64-bit
Extract the ENTIRE ZIP into a NEW folder, then run Asterfall.exe.
Java is included. Keep lib/, assets/, content/, and runtime/ together.
If launch fails, run Play-with-log.bat and share launch-log.txt.

THIS BUILD FOCUSES ON PRESENTATION AND PLAYER FEEL
One compact garden: one sword-and-shield hero, one enemy type, one destructible
crate, one chest, one building, raised ground and a traversable ramp. No new
quests, item definitions, classes, enemy types or progression content.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrows     Direct movement relative to camera
Left click        Approach / attack
Shift + LMB       Stand and slash
Right mouse       Walk toward cursor (direct steering, no navmesh)
Space             Eased dodge, brief invulnerability
Q / E             Retained Sword Wave / Whirlwind
F                 Collect loot / open chest / rest near entrance
R                 Potion
I / K             Existing equipment / talents menus
Esc               Pause and settings
Mouse wheel       Adjust camera distance
N                 Reset the practice garden
F1                Field guide

WHAT CHANGED
Close perspective camera; larger player; damped camera follow; acceleration,
braking and turning; blended stride/idle; sword anticipation/contact/recovery;
attack lunges; hit-stop; recoil/knockback; trails, flashes, particles and sound.
Toon lighting, rim light, softened sun shadows, screen-space ambient occlusion,
contact darkening, fog, grading and subtle bloom. Cool nights have warm lights.
The HUD is compact and garden notes collapse. Loot labels appear nearby, use
rarity colors and avoid each other and the player. World drops bob and glow.

YOUR LOCAL SKY
Weather defaults to approximate ZIP 11355 (Flushing, Queens, NY).
Your PC's clock/time zone determines local time. Open-Meteo supplies current
weather and sunrise/sunset; refreshed every 15 minutes while open. Offline uses
last known conditions with status shown in Settings. No account or API key.
No precise GPS access. Settings accepts a city or latitude, longitude.

SAVES / UPDATES
Version-1 saves remain compatible in %USERPROFILE%\.asterfall\save-v1.json.
A previous-save backup is kept alongside it. Equipment, bag, level, gold,
talents and location persist. Exact scene positions and prop states reset.
Back up .asterfall before updates and always extract updates into a new folder.
The older expedition/boss loop is not the focus of this build; N resets the
single test area so you can judge movement and combat repeatedly.

VALIDATION / LIMITS
186 movement/layout checks + 28 existing checks, actual graphics-context combat
regression (including 60 projectile level-ups), terrain/camera checks, and real
day/night/motion captures. Windows launcher is cross-built and still needs
human Windows/GPU playtesting. Requires OpenGL 2.0 with framebuffer objects and
24-bit depth renderbuffers. The executable is unsigned.

This is a lightweight toon pipeline, not full metallic/roughness PBR.
Source and architecture notes are in the separate source archive/repository.
Third-party licenses and runtime notices are included. Weather: Open-Meteo
(CC BY 4.0) and GeoNames. Character meshes, textures, music and effects are
original procedural content; no Dragon Quest artwork or models used.
