Version 12 draws on the supplied references as a group:

- Zelda 3: readable overhead navigation, clear silhouettes and directional movement.
- Chrono Trigger town and menu references: layered scenery, a central landmark, expressive action poses and legible RPG information.
- Painted village and cave references: dimensional 2D scenes, warm light, sculpted rocks and organic contours.
- Both fountain town screenshots: rounded houses, branching sandy paths, lush canopy edges, a winged fountain and contact shadows.
- Orange-haired character sheet: expressive four-direction poses, clear weight shifts and readable clothing folds. The new traveler has copper hair, a teal coat, orange scarf, slate trousers and boots.
- Cottage and peaceful grass screenshot: rounded blue plaster, soft thatch, greenery around entrances, broad quiet ground colors and sparse ground accents.
- Dialogue references: parchment, warm wood and a framed portrait. Traditional clock hands remain.

Keep the grass quiet, avoid glitter and star particles, and make depth through shading and overlap. Keep paths and collision boundaries readable. Retain the current movement, sword mechanics, four maps, New York time and Flushing weather when changing art.

## Eventual human art handoff

Commission a character animator for idle/walk/run/attack in four directions, an environment artist for ground, water, trees, cliffs and buildings, a prop artist for landmarks and enemies, and a UI/portrait artist. An art director can unify scale, light and palette. Give them this file, assets/README.md and the current metadata tables. Agree in writing on usage rights, source-file delivery, credit, animation counts and commercial redistribution. Record authors, contracts and licenses alongside replacement assets. Familiar reference styles do not grant rights to reuse their characters or assets.

The current images are AI-generated test assets; this document is a replacement checklist, not a claim of human authorship or copyright clearance.
