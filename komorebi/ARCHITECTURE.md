# Code map

- `Desktop` configures the libGDX LWJGL3 desktop window.
- `NeighborhoodGame` coordinates lifecycle, updating, shadow and world rendering, screenshots, and the small controls overlay.
- `NeighborhoodScene` composes the static residential area and registers solid footprints. `Geometry` batches custom meshes by material and splits them before the 16-bit vertex limit.
- `Materials` owns textures and the palette. Textures are deterministic authored assets generated by `make_assets.py`; rendering does not run Python.
- `Player` owns the temporary articulated character and walking animation. `PlayerMotor` handles continuous motion separately from graphics.
- `InputController` maps keys into camera-relative normalized world directions.
- `CollisionWorld` resolves a circular player against rectangular footprints and circular tree/prop footprints, substepping motion and sliding along walls. Units are meters on the X/Z plane; Y is elevation.
- `CameraController` owns projection, fixed heading, velocity lead, and exponential following.
- `LightingController` owns the chosen afternoon environment and shadow-casting sun. A later clock can change this controller without changing entity placement or input.
- `SceneRenderer` supplies a wider nine-sample shadow filter to libGDX's standard material shader.
- `MovementChecks` runs deterministic movement and collision behavior checks without an OpenGL window.

No ECS, world tile grid, gameplay framework, or inventory/combat scaffolding is introduced. Static facades are not enterable; terrain and movement remain on one walkable elevation for this milestone. Character limbs are articulated model instances rather than a skeletal animation dependency. Foliage and surfaces are temporary authored art.
